# Privacy Policy: As I Recall

Last updated: 1 November 2026

As I Recall is an Android app made by SpareTimeProduct for keeping private notes about the people, places and services in your life. Everything you write stays on your phone. We do not run any server that the app talks to, and we receive no data from it.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your notes and details.** The notes, fragments, people, accounts and places you create, with any dates, amounts, photos and PDFs you add. They are kept only on your device, in a database encrypted with a key held in Android's secure storage. Photos and PDFs are encrypted on the device too.
- **Photos.** When you add a photo, the app re-saves it without its metadata (location, device model, time taken). A photo in a format the app cannot re-save is kept as it is, but still encrypted.
- **Settings.** Your currency, appearance, lock and backup choices.
- **Automatic backups.** About once a day the app writes an encrypted snapshot of your data to its own private storage on your phone and keeps the few most recent. These are not shared or uploaded.

## Access to your phone

- **No internet access.** The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send anything over a network.
- **Contacts.** The app does not have permission to read your contacts. If you turn on the contacts feature, you pick one contact at a time in Android's own contact picker, and the app receives only that contact's name. "Save to phone contacts" opens Android's own screen with the details filled in, and you decide whether to save.
- **Fingerprint or face unlock.** The optional lock uses Android's biometric prompt. The app never sees your fingerprint or face, only whether Android accepted it.
- **Photos, camera and files.** The app opens Android's camera, gallery or file picker only when you ask it to attach something, and receives only what you choose.

## Backups and sharing

Exporting a backup is something you start. It creates a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and you pick where it goes using Android's share sheet. If you forget the passphrase, nobody, including us, can open the file. Sharing a single note also goes through the share sheet, to the app you choose. Once a file leaves the app, the app you sent it to handles it under its own policy.

The app turns off Android's cloud backup, so your data is not copied to your Google account by Android.

## What the app does not do

- It does not send data to us or to any third party.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
- It does not need an account.
- It does not load code or fonts from the network.
- It does not sell or share data.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting a note, fragment, person, account or place in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, including automatic backups. We hold no copy, so we cannot recover or delete your data for you. Backup files you exported yourself stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose notebook and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed before the new version is released.
