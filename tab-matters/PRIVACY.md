# Privacy Policy: Tab Matters

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Tab Matters is a Chrome extension made by SpareTimeProduct that jumps you to
your open tabs by domain. It runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate
any server that Tab Matters talks to, and we receive no data from it.

## What Tab Matters accesses

- **Your open tabs' addresses and titles** (the `tabs` permission). The extension
  reads them only when you use the shortcut, click the icon, or open the Multi
  Mode panel, to find which tabs match the domains in your list and to show their
  titles and icons. It does not read the content of any page, and it does not read
  or keep your browsing history.
- **Your settings and domain list** (the `storage` permission). The domains you
  add, their labels, groups, order, on/off state and optional "open if no tab"
  addresses, plus your Multi Mode and Dark Mode choices, are saved with Chrome's
  `storage.sync`. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google syncs
  them between your devices as part of your own Google account. We cannot see
  them.

## What Tab Matters does not do

- It does not send any data to us or to any third party.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or error-reporting services.
- It does not load code from the network.
- It does not sell or share data.
- It only opens addresses that start with `http://` or `https://`.

## Import and export

Export saves your domain list as a file on your device. Import reads a file you
choose, in your browser. Neither sends anything over the network.

## Data retention

Tab Matters keeps only your settings and domain list, until you remove them or
uninstall the extension. Removing the extension deletes the local copy; Chrome
removes synced copies according to your Google account's sync settings.

## Changes to this policy

If the way Tab Matters handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and
the date above changed before the new version is released.
